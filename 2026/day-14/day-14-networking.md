How does this wifi works?

\-> Physical internet

\-> router -> Connects your home or office to the internet. It takes incoming data packets from the ISP and routes them to the correct device (phone, laptop, etc.).

\-> switch -> used in data center, A physical device mainly used in data centers or offices. It connects multiple devices

\-> hub -> A simpler, older device that connects multiple devices but sends packets to all connected devices (broadcast). Unlike a switch, it doesn’t filter or direct traffic efficiently.
